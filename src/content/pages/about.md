## About this site

Every file in `src/content/pages/` becomes a page at its own address: `about.md` is served at `/about/`, and `notes/uses.org` would be served at `/notes/uses/`. Pages can be Markdown, MDX or Org, just like posts, but they don't appear in the list of posts or the feed.
